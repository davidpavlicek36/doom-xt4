doom-xt4

XTeink X4 · ESP32-C3 · e-ink raycaster

1 — Connect

  1. Plug in the USB-C cable
  2. Turn on the device

2 — Flash

Requires Chrome or Edge on desktop.
Firefox and Safari do not support Web Serial.

Flashing takes ~30 seconds. Do not unplug during the process.

3 — Restart

  1. Quickly press and release Reset, then hold Power
  2. The device will boot into the game
